IMPORTANT SAFETY DESIGN
- Direct product pages are used for stable WOTC patterns and manually checked chase cards.
- Team Rocket and other variant-sensitive pages do NOT get guessed. They use Cardmarket's exact
  collector-number search syntax, e.g. "Dark Blastoise TR3".
- Unknown cards also use set-code search instead of a fabricated product URL.
- NM/EX/GD/PL/PO and language parameters are appended to every generated URL.

LIMITATION
Cardmarket can rename or split product variants later. No external app can honestly guarantee a
permanent 10000% match against future Cardmarket changes without using Cardmarket's current product
catalog/IDs at runtime. v61 is therefore fail-safe: it never guesses a direct product page for
variant-sensitive or unknown records; it falls back to the official collector-code search.
